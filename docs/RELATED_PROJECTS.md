# Related projects

Visitors use flyer-builder to turn editable, coordinate-free flyer descriptions and local artwork
into a one-page PDF. These projects support related document-authoring workflows.

## Confirmed related projects

### djot-slide-builder

- Relationship: Prior implementation.
- Link: https://github.com/vosslab/djot-slide-builder
- Why visitors may care: It builds source-editable, lecture-ready presentations and PDF exports
  from Djot when a presentation, rather than a one-page flyer, is the needed output.
- Evidence: The repository describes its Djot-to-editable-presentation and PDF pipeline; its StVC
  flyer informed this repository's first migrated flyer.

### brickarchitect-label-converter

- Relationship: Prior implementation.
- Link: https://github.com/vosslab/brickarchitect-label-converter
- Why visitors may care: It is a ReportLab PDF workflow for printable labels, useful as a more
  specialized example of programmatic print layout.
- Evidence: The project describes converting Brother P-touch LBX labels into Avery PDF sheets.

## Possible related projects

### Typst

- Relationship: Direct alternative.
- Link: https://typst.app/
- Why visitors may care: Typst is another source-based way to create automated, print-ready
  documents, and suits projects needing its document markup and typesetting features.
- Evidence: Its official site describes structured markup, automatic formatting, and PDF export.
- Confidence: Likely.
